## The line

> I promise to love you through every update, every crash, and every 2% battery warning — till firmware do us part.

One original one-line robot wedding vow, with the twist landing on the
last phrase: the robot promises love through every update, every crash,
and every 2% battery warning — the three things machines dread most —
then closes on the wedding classic, rewritten in machine terms: "till
firmware do us part." The setup is a sincere vow; the payoff is that a
robot's idea of "for better or worse" is measured in software updates
and battery percentages, and it means every word of it anyway.
